My Dearest Jelaica,

Happy 2nd Monthsary, my bebeee. ❤️

Happy 2nd monthsary to us. Two months may seem like a short time, but within those two months, we have already shared so many memories, laughter, misunderstandings, and moments that I will always treasure.

I know our relationship has never been perfect. There have been moments when I have hurt you, times when I failed to express myself properly, and days when understanding each other felt difficult. But despite everything, please know that my love and appreciation for you have never become any less.

Sometimes, I feel sad when I think about the possibility that one day, things between us might not be the same anymore. Perhaps it is because you have already become such an important part of my life. You are someone I look forward to talking to, someone I want to share my days with, and someone I sincerely hope I can keep in my life for a very long time.

I cannot promise that I will always be perfect, Bebe, but I can promise that I will continue learning and becoming a better person. I want to grow not only for myself but also for us. Whenever we face problems, I do not want to simply give up. I want to learn how to listen, understand, and do everything I can to make things right.

Thank you for staying by my side, Bebe, for understanding me even when I struggle to express what I truly feel, and for giving me moments that I will forever cherish. Every conversation, every smile, every simple gesture, and every moment we have shared has become deeply meaningful to me. Sometimes, I quietly wish that time would slow down because I know that every beautiful moment with you is something I can never hold onto forever. And perhaps that is what makes loving you so precious to me—the fear that one day, I might have to live without the person who has become such an important part of my life.

Bebe, I love you more than I can properly express through words. Even on the days when I fail to show it, please remember that my feelings for you are sincere and genuine. You have given me memories and emotions that I will always carry in my heart.

I sincerely hope that these two months are only the beginning of the many months and beautiful memories we will continue to share, Bebe. I hope we can face every challenge together, even when things become difficult and uncertain. Someday, when we look back on these first two months, I hope we will remember not only the happiness we shared but also the difficult moments that tested us. And if there ever comes a time when everything feels heavy, I hope we will still choose to understand, cherish, and fight for what we have, because despite all the sadness and uncertainties, having you in my life is something I will always be grateful for.

No matter how difficult the days may become, I hope you will always remember how much you mean to me. I am grateful for every moment, every memory, and every second I have had with you.

Happy 2nd Monthsary, my Bebe.
I love you more than words can properly express. ❤️

Forever yours,
Anghel
